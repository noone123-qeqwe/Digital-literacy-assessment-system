# Research approval pack — DRAFT, not approved

Prepared 16 November 2026 for College review. This document does not authorize research collection. The deployment remains `DLAS_RESEARCH_ENABLED=false`; approval references, contacts, and consent flags must not be invented.

## Basis and changes requiring review

Source documents supplied by the project owner: **APPENDICES THS.docx**, Appendix B; **QUESTIONNAIRE Ths.docx**; and **THS 1-5 Final.docx**, Data Gathering Procedure. Appendix B provides adult consent, minor assent, and guardian consent. The thesis describes a printed, in-person questionnaire of approximately 20 minutes, voluntary participation, no grade consequences, and access limited to researchers and their adviser.

The website changes that procedure: accounts identify participants, email services carry verification messages, a cloud database stores responses, and a browser can retain unsynchronized answers. College reviewers should approve these differences before data collection. Account-linked responses are **confidential and pseudonymized in reporting**, rather than anonymous to every system operator. A typed name and checkbox do not establish equivalence to a signed paper consent form.

The original research instrument remains thirty self-assessment items in five DigComp competence areas, using the approved 1–4 response scale. Scored knowledge quizzes, percentages, and achievement certificates are a separate educational workflow and must not be substituted for thesis scores without an approved protocol change.

## Proposed minor assent

**DRAFT — College approval required.** Explain the study in age-appropriate language. Ask the student to confirm they understand the task, can ask questions, can decline, and can stop without consequences for grades or standing. Obtain assent separately from the guardian's permission. Guardian permission must not override a student's refusal.

## Proposed guardian procedure

**DRAFT — College approval required; email verification alone is insufficient to establish guardian identity.**

1. Authorized College staff check the guardian relationship through an approved institutional record or the College-approved signed consent process. Use an approved contact channel, rather than relying solely on details entered by the student.
2. Record the minimum evidence needed in the approved register: participant code, reviewer, date, method, and evidence reference. Do not collect government-ID images by default.
3. Send the guardian the approved notice and a one-time verification link through the approved channel. Explain that clicking a link proves access to that email address, rather than independently proving legal guardianship.
4. Retain the signed form or equivalent approved evidence under the retention policy. Record the guardian's informed decision and the minor's separate assent.
5. Permit the research attempt only after all required steps are complete. Declines, pending reviews, and withdrawals must not be treated as consent.

The software currently records guardian information and email verification. The College must decide how verified identity evidence is supplied, retained, and reviewed; that decision must be implemented and tested before research is enabled.

The operational computer backup currently uses a provisional thirty-day rotation. That setting is not an approved research retention rule. Provider-managed recovery and backup expiry must be covered by the final deletion policy.

## Release gate

Keep research collection disabled until signed approval is recorded, the contact and retention fields are complete, the guardian process is accepted and implemented, a restore rehearsal passes, and responsible College staff review the participant-facing wording. Re-run the research readiness checks after configuration. Leave the original instrument and its scoring rules unchanged unless an approved versioned amendment says otherwise.
